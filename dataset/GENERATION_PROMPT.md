# Batch instructions

Choose independently distinct scenarios under the domain taxonomy. Give each example a stable slug and retain its split group for every variant. Track deficits by capability, language style and domain before deciding what to author next. Read the exact allowlisted source sections first. Record their provenance, dates if available, rights scope and hash. When the rights or grounding are unresolved, hold the record outside the candidate release.

Write varied spoken questions in English, native Telugu, Romanized Telugu and Telugu-English code-switching. Use natural incomplete utterances and follow-ups. An example should have a distinct conversational goal, clinical context or decision point. Duplicate wording and mechanically substituted scenarios should be rejected.

Use supplied passages for grounded examples. Include examples with conflicting claims or insufficient context, and express the uncertainty. Use authoritative source passages for concrete medical claims, preserving conditions and warning signs. Keep answers concise. For a time-critical emergency, put the immediate action first. Give useful clarification in non-emergency cases without diagnosing or prescribing. Include only the final answer, no hidden reasoning traces.

For RAG, draft one coherent English passage per topic facet and an equivalent Telugu passage. Preserve quantities, negations, conditional statements and urgency. Prefer 100–250 model tokens when appropriate. If a source excerpt contains a country-specific number, use verified local guidance for that audience or phrase the action as calling their local emergency services.

Append accepted records to the authoring TSV inputs described in the handoff. Record batch-level source and teacher details for subsequent releases. Run structural and tokenizer checks, inspect near-matches, then review new claims and translation fidelity against the cited source. Capture failures and edit or remove the affected rows. Record qualified review separately. Maintain a factual account of completed records and shortfalls.
